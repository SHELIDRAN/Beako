Neko for Windows 0.2.0 adds MangaDex sign-in, much richer search, a fully adjustable reader, and frosted glass throughout.

## Download

Download `Neko-0.2.0-windows-x64.exe` below and run it. It's a single self-contained file with no installer and needs 64-bit Windows 10 or 11. Your library and settings from 0.1.0 carry over.

Windows SmartScreen may warn about an unrecognized app because the file isn't code-signed. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. You can check the download against the `.sha256` file.

## New

**MangaDex account.** Sign in with a personal API client (Settings → MangaDex Account). The titles you follow are added to your Library, reading statuses appear on title pages and in Library filters, and chapters you read are marked read on MangaDex. Your password is never stored, and the client secret is kept in Windows Credential Manager.

**Search and filters.**
- A Filters sheet for MangaDex based on Comix's tag set: genres, formats, types (Manga, Manhwa, Manhua), statuses, demographics, and sort orders, plus MangaDex themes. Click a tag once to include it or twice to exclude it, and choose whether titles need any or all of the included tags.
- A new MangaBaka catalog with its own filters: minimum rating, minimum chapters, publication years, anime adaptation, licensing, and sorts such as Trending This Week. Click a MangaBaka title and Neko finds it on MangaDex.

**Reader.**
- Page sizes: Fit Screen, Fit Width, Fit Height, and Original.
- Two-page spreads, always or automatic, with the cover shown alone.
- Zoom with Ctrl + wheel, `+` / `-`, or double-click, and drag to pan.
- Crop Borders, Dim Pages, and a black, gray, or white background.
- Strip width and page spacing for webtoons.
- Swappable click zones, wheel page turns, auto-continue to the next chapter, read-ahead, a control auto-hide delay, an always-visible page number, and keeping the screen on.
- Press `S`, or click the sliders button, to change all of these without leaving the page.

**Design.**
- The window uses Windows 11 Mica (Acrylic on Windows 10), so the sidebar is translucent glass.
- The reader's controls are frosted glass that picks up the page's colors.
- Title pages sit on a soft wash of the cover's colors.
- A Continue Reading shelf in the Library.
- Covers fade in, and the filter chips, sliders, and switches follow Apple's style.
