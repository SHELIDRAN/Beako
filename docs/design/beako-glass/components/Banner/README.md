# Banner

A notice across the top of a screen, with optional actions on the right.

- Full width, `rim` glass, `radius-panel`, padding 10 × `space-lg`, `space-md` gaps; an `icon-md` ErrorOutline in `text-muted`, message `body-medium` in `text`.
- **Use for** a state that applies to the whole screen and that Beako has already handled — chiefly the MangaDex fallback ("Showing MangaDex results — Comick is unavailable" / "…found no matches") with **Try Comick again**, or "Comick unreachable" with **Retry**.
- One sentence; say what happened and what Beako did. At most two actions.
