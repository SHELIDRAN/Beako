Beako is a manga reader for Windows, based on the [Neko](https://github.com/nekomangaorg/Neko) Android app (Apache-2.0).

## Beako 0.1.12

**Install:** on 0.1.9 or later, choose **Update now** when Beako offers this version (or More → General → About → Check for updates). Otherwise download `Beako-0.1.12-windows-x64.msi` below and run it; it upgrades earlier versions in place and keeps your library and settings. The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may warn you: choose *More info → Run anyway*. The `.sha256` file lets you check the download.

### New
- **Continue where you left off, on every title.** Beako now remembers read chapters and your page for titles outside your library too, so their title page offers **Continue Chapter N** at the right page. Adding a title to your library keeps its progress; places read before this update come from your Reading history.
- **Resume opens the chapter.** In Reading history, **Resume** goes straight into the reader at your chapter and page.
- **A synopsis on every title page.** When a title arrives without one (from charts, history or a source), Beako looks it up on Comick or MangaDex. Library titles keep it for next time.

### Improved
- **Read chapters stand out:** they're faded with a tick, and the chapter in progress shows "Reading · page X of Y" with a small progress bar.

Beako uses comick.dev only for discovery and tracking: it never reads chapters or images from Comick. Not affiliated with Comick, MangaDex, Mangayomi or Neko. The extension bridge and site templates are adapted from [Mangayomi](https://github.com/kodjodevf/mangayomi) and its extensions (Apache-2.0). Country flags from [flag-icons](https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons) (MIT).
