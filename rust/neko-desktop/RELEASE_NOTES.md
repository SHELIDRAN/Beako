The first release of Neko for Windows: a native desktop MangaDex reader written in Rust and designed in Apple's design language.

## Download

Download `Neko-0.1.0-windows-x64.exe` below and run it. It's a single self-contained file with no installer. It needs 64-bit Windows 10 or 11.

Windows SmartScreen may warn about an unrecognized app because the file isn't code-signed. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. You can check the download against the `.sha256` file.

## What's included

- **Browse** MangaDex by Popular, Latest, New, or Top Rated, or search by title.
- **Title details** with the full chapter list in your language, with read and unread marks. Right-click a chapter to mark it read or unread.
- **Reader** with left-to-right, right-to-left, and vertical scroll modes. Use the arrow keys, Space, or the mouse wheel to turn pages, `[` and `]` to change chapters, `F` for full screen, and Esc to close.
- **Library** that remembers your place in every title.
- **Settings** for chapter language, Data Saver, the default reading mode, and content filters.
- Light and dark appearance that follows your Windows setting.

## Not included yet

MangaDex login and follows sync, trackers, merged sources, offline downloads, and backups from the Android app.

Your library is stored in `%APPDATA%\Neko\Neko Desktop\data\library.json`.
